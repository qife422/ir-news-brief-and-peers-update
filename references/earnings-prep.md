# Earnings preparation

Use `--mode earnings` after reading `workspace-schema.md`. Use only authorized company disclosures and supplied/authorized market-expectation snapshots. The report may remain useful as a Q&A preparation pack when reliable consensus is unavailable.

## Inputs and comparisons

Each financial value needs a source excerpt, company, metric, period, unit, currency, accounting/adjusted convention and share/ADS basis. A consensus snapshot also needs its timestamp before the actual result was first publicly released. Filing availability time must not be substituted for the original earnings-release time when checking whether consensus is pre-release.

Compare exactly matching definitions. Revenue and gross bookings, GAAP and adjusted EPS, different fiscal quarters, currencies, shares/ADS or constant-currency conventions are not interchangeable. Keep conflicting originals available for review; do not silently normalize a conflicting definition.

The script calculates numeric differences deterministically. Missing inputs remain unavailable, not zero. Zero consensus has no meaningful percentage denominator. For negative consensus, show the absolute difference and a clearly qualified relative calculation if implemented; do not mechanically call a smaller loss a miss based on a signed denominator. Metric direction must be explicit: a cost decline is not automatically bad.

## Q&A pack

For each important topic, prepare the likely opening question, one or more follow-ups, a short evidence-backed answer and missing information. Separate published company wording from the draft. Where company guidance has not been provided, describe the preparation need rather than inventing a forecast.

Use a versioned answer with statement-level evidence. Review binds the precise answer text and supporting evidence. A text edit, new material version, withdrawal, changed access scope or expired cutoff can require re-review. Human confirmation is an audit record, not a substitute for checking evidence.

The output supports management rehearsal and review. It does not publish answers, supply legal conclusions or claim that all investor questions will be covered. After results, compare actual questions with the pre-result frozen pack and keep pre-filing evidence separate from information learned later.
