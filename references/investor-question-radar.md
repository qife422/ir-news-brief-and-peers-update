# Investor question radar

Use for a new radar or an update to existing topics. Read `workspace-schema.md` for the executable input format. Keep the company, peer set, cutoff, material versions, output audience and prior snapshot explicit.

## Build a useful topic

Select a concrete business path using `professional-methodology.md`: demand, supply, economics, competition or valuation. Initial categories can include AI/distribution, international growth, China travel demand, marketing/conversion, supply/pricing, revenue/bookings, margins, direct booking, capital allocation and FX/policy. Empty categories need no invented content.

Author one topic around a decision or investor concern. Include supported facts, conditional analysis, a strong counterpoint, candidate questions and follow-ups, prior company wording, answer preparation, gaps and a specific action. Facts and company wording point to exact source excerpts; conditional analysis identifies its premises. A competitor's cost improvement can support a question about the subject company, not a claim that its costs already improved.

Do not equate topic priority with question probability or share-price effect. Use the existing materiality framework, a visible change reason and an explicit preparation deadline. Confirmed actual questions may inform priority only after eligible meeting feedback is available. Explain any manual ranking adjustment.

## Time, change and history

Use a timezone-aware `as_of`. Distinguish publication, version availability, access time and report time. If a historical page has changed, use a fixed filing/version or mark the earlier content unavailable. Do not label unknown dates as current.

An initial report is a baseline. Subsequent reports compare stable topic/claim IDs and supported content with the previous projected report; explain additions, removed support and revised questions. An inaccessible or retracted source is not a negative business event. Keep evidence changes separate from changes in business performance.

Use snapshots and events to retain input identity and version history. Do not overwrite an old answer merely to keep an accepted-looking status. Retraction invalidates dependent claims and answer reviews; a genuinely independent support group must support the entire same statement before it can preserve the claim.

## Human review

Inspect every important fact against its cited passage, including company identity, quarter, units and any quoted wording. Review the transmission path and alternative explanation. Record missing evidence and who needs to prepare it; do not manufacture management guidance or investor intent.

A good radar lets an IR reviewer understand what changed, why the question matters and what must be prepared, without reopening the whole news history. Hand over the source list and projected report; retain only the minimum authorized excerpts, not licensed raw exports.
