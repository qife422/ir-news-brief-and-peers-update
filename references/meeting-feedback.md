# Meeting preparation and feedback

Use `--mode meeting` after reading `workspace-schema.md`. Start with manually supplied and authorized records. Do not automatically record calls, scrape private contacts, write CRM records or contact investors.

## Before a meeting

Build a brief from applicable reviewed or explicitly Draft answers, the current topic radar, prior confirmed questions and unresolved follow-ups. The absence of meeting history should be visible. An inferred interest is not a statement of a fund's strategy, holdings or intention.

## After a meeting

Attach each actual question to the meeting identity and an exact authorized record excerpt. Keep AI-predicted questions, simulations and second-hand reports labeled separately. The source passage establishes that the question was asked; a human confirmation records who checked it and when.

Deduplicate repeated imports of the same meeting question. Confirmation and revocation are append-only events written to a new workspace. An unconfirmed or revoked record must not increase actual-question counts or influence priority as if confirmed. Preserve prior records for audit; correcting a record does not authorize erasing the underlying input.

Record follow-up content, owner and timing only when supplied or assigned by the user. A proposed action remains proposed. Meetings containing private people or internal information require the permitted processing environment; Example fixtures do not demonstrate that a real company's privacy permissions exist.

## Output and review

Public reports exclude private meeting content and text derived solely from it. Internal reports still require authorization and the intended audience. Report projected content rather than hiding private DOM elements. Review the meeting brief and pending tasks; external sending and official wording require the user's corresponding instruction.
